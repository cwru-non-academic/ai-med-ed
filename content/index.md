## Current Focus Areas

### **Assessment & Evaluation**
Automated grading systems for clinical documentation, OSCE encounters, and standardized assessments. Projects include SOAP note evaluation, multiple-choice question generation, and comprehensive feedback platforms.

### **Curriculum Development** 
AI-powered curriculum mapping, learning objective enhancement, and content alignment systems that connect educational activities with accreditation standards and competency frameworks.

### **Institutional Analytics**
Data integration platforms for continuous quality improvement (CQI), accreditation reporting, and predictive modeling to support evidence-based educational decisions.

### **Student Support & Learning**
Personalized tutoring systems, research assistance platforms, and adaptive learning technologies that enhance student success and engagement.

### **Admissions Innovation**
Comprehensive analytics platforms for applicant screening, yield prediction, and holistic evaluation processes that support fair and effective student selection.

## Key Stakeholders & Collaborators

**Center for Medical Education Leadership** at Case Western Reserve University, including leadership in assessment, program evaluation, innovation, and research. External partnerships span Cleveland Clinic Lerner College, University Hospitals, and interdisciplinary collaborations with engineering, data science, and cognitive science faculty.

## About This Site

This workspace serves as a dynamic knowledge base for tracking emerging research, developing project concepts, and coordinating stakeholder engagement. Content is continuously updated to reflect the evolving landscape of AI applications in medical education.

The site uses an interconnected structure where literature informs project development, stakeholder insights shape implementation strategies, and practical resources support successful deployment of AI innovations in medical education settings.
