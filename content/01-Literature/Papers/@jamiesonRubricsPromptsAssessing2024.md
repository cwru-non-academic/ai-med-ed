# Notes
>Case study: OSCE post-encounter note grading
>Reduced human effort by 91% (measured by gradeable items), reduced turnaround
>
>Zero-shot LLM w/ minimal prompt engineering
>89.7% agreement with human expert graders.
>Smaller Llama 2.7b can be fine-tuned, which helps scale things down.

> Their procedure: "rubrics to prompts". Each rubric itembs becomes a yes/no claim-verification task

Super important paper with helpful recipes and feedback
