# Notes
>- George Washington: 
>	- third- and fourth-year students a two-week elective, [Artificial Intelligence Applications in Healthcare](https://smhs.gwu.edu/course/artificial-intelligence-applications-healthcare). The course includes lectures and case studies about using AI for research and patient care.
>	- (Ioannis Koutroulis, MD, PhD, associate dean of admissions)
>- Harvard: 
>	- AI in Medicine PhD
>	- Computationally Enabled Medicine, uses AI and other tools to analyze biomedical data, such as genomics and epidemiology
>- UVA: 
>	- AI helps dx and tx ficticious pts 
>	- students in a foundational clinical course learn how to use AI along with other resources to help diagnose patient illnesses and develop treatment plans,
>	- (Andrew Parsons, MD, associate professor of medicine and associate dean for clinical competency)
>- UTexas San Antonio: 
>	- dual degrees MD/AI
>	- first- and second-year students two electives that introduce them to generative AI and to the programming language behind it.
>	- ethics-focused course called Medicine, Behavior, and Society includes a 90-minute presentation about generative AI,
>- Stanford: 
>	- New AI curriculum (Jonathan Chen MD PHD, Dir MedEd in Ai).
>- Miami:
>	- The MedEdPORTAL module in [[@agarwalExploringApplicationsArtificial2025]]
>- Mt Sinai:
>	- will provide all medical and graduate students with access to OpenAI’s ChatGPT Edu (a subscription-based version of ChatGPT that focuses on educational needs) and train them in how to use it.
