# Notes
>
>- LLMS - impressive clinical performance
>- Reasoning models can help open the 'black box'
>- Questions remain about what "reasoning" means with LLMs
>- Vulnerable to buzzwords and "frequently co-occurring clinical features"
>- need benchmarks to challenge reasoning process

A real reasoning model can
- teach, grade, discuss in cases
- assist with learning complicated material
- develop new patient presentation
- improve communication in standardized patient interactions
- assist with licensing prep
- answer student questions

students and physicians must
- act as a responsible stewards
- provide ovesight with human-level reasoning and interpretation skills
- learn to appraise, contextualize, and safely integrate output
- have data systems literacy
