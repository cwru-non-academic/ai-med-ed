Chief of Staff, SoM

Elizabeth Fehsenfeld, DMH, serves as the Chief of Staff at CWRU School of Medicine collaborating with Dean Stan Gerson since April 2022. In this role she serves as an extender representing Dr. Gerson when he is not able to be in two places at once, she serves on university committees representing the SOM, serves as liaison for the university-wide initiatives including the Population and Community Health Initiative. She works with the other Vice Deans and Chairs of the School while overseeing the areas of Human Resources, Dean’s Administration, monitors the Strategic Plan, while also taking on “other duties as assigned.”
