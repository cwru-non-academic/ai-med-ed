## Germ of the Idea

Automated grading and feedback system for various assignments and reflections including Inquiry & Quality (IQ), Team Skills (TS), Portfolio submissions, and other written work.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Grading assignments and reflections is time-intensive, and providing meaningful feedback on written work creates significant faculty workload.

### Proposed Solution

AI system capable of grading and providing feedback on diverse assignment types, from structured reports to reflective writing, with customizable rubrics.

### Success Metrics

- Reduction in grading time
- Quality of feedback provided
- Consistency across different evaluators

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Large Language Models for text analysis
- Rubric-based assessment algorithms
- Feedback generation systems

### Data Requirements

- Historical assignments with grades
- Grading rubrics for different assignment types
- Faculty feedback examples

### Integration Points

*Note: These integration requirements are provisional and will be refined based on actual institutional systems and capabilities.*

- Student portfolio and assignment management system
- Course content delivery and assignment distribution platform
- Student work submission and tracking system
