## Germ of the Idea

Comprehensive AI-powered institutional analytics platform that consolidates and visualizes data from multiple sources (survey results, accreditation reports, student assessments) with specialized CQI dashboard for LCME compliance, generating interactive insights, trend analysis, and automated reporting for data-driven program improvement and accreditation management.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical education programs generate vast amounts of data across multiple systems and formats, making it difficult to gain comprehensive insights for program improvement and LCME compliance. Manual data consolidation is time-intensive, prone to errors, and often results in delayed or fragmented reporting that hinders nimble decision-making. Programs struggle to maintain comprehensive oversight of accreditation requirements due to fragmented systems, manual reporting processes, and difficulty identifying emerging compliance issues.

### Proposed Solution

Integrated AI platform that: (1) automatically extracts and consolidates data from disparate sources, (2) integrates and standardizes data formats, (3) generates interactive dashboards with real-time updates, (4) provides specialized LCME compliance monitoring, (5) identifies trends and patterns through machine learning, (6) produces natural language summaries of insights, (7) enables rapid identification of improvement opportunities, and (8) generates real-time, LCME-aligned reports for proactive quality management.

### Success Metrics

- Reduction in time from data collection to actionable insights
- Improved accuracy of integrated reporting
- Increased frequency of data-driven decision making
- Enhanced identification of program improvement opportunities
- Improved LCME compliance monitoring accuracy
- Reduction in time to identify and address quality issues
- Decreased preparation time for accreditation reporting
- Enhanced early warning system effectiveness
- User satisfaction with dashboard accessibility and insights
