## Germ of the Idea

Develop an AI tool that automates literature tracking and analysis using web scraping, NLP, and topic modeling to scan research databases, summarize publications, categorize trends, and map research gaps to specific medical education topics of interest to identify viable research projects.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical education research literature is expanding rapidly, making it difficult to track emerging trends, identify research gaps, and connect findings to specific areas of institutional interest. Manual literature review is time-intensive and may miss important connections between studies.

### Proposed Solution

Comprehensive AI system that: (1) automatically scans and scrapes research databases, (2) summarizes new publications using NLP, (3) categorizes emerging trends through topic modeling, (4) integrates with citation managers, (5) identifies research gaps, and (6) maps gaps to 10-12 predefined medical education focus areas to suggest viable research projects.

### Success Metrics

- Number of relevant papers automatically identified and processed
- Quality of research gap identification
- Successful mapping to institutional research priorities
- Time reduction in literature review processes
- Number of viable research projects identified

### AI/ML Components Needed
