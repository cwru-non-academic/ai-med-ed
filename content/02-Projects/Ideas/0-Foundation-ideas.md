## [[Research - Medical Education Research Gaps]]

Develop an AI tool that automates literature tracking and analysis. Using web scraping, NLP, and topic modeling, the system could scan research databases, summarize new publications, and categorize emerging trends. It could also integrate with citation managers to assist researchers in identifying gaps in the literature and formulating new research questions. Map these gaps to medical education topics that are areas of interest (10-12) to our collective group to identify projects that may be amendable to research projects.

## [[Institutional Analytics - Data Integration and CQI Dashboard]]

Develop a comprehensive AI-powered institutional analytics platform that consolidates and visualizes data from multiple sources, including survey results, accreditation reports, and student assessments and evaluations. The system includes specialized CQI dashboard for LCME compliance, generating interactive insights, trend analysis, and automated reporting for data-driven program improvement and accreditation management.
