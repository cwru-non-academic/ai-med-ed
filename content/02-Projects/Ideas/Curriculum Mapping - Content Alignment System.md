## Germ of the Idea

Comprehensive AI system for mapping curriculum content to external standards (USMLE Step 1) and internal assessments (SSEQ questions), ensuring proper alignment between what is taught, what is assessed, and what is required for licensing examinations.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical schools struggle to ensure comprehensive coverage of USMLE Step 1 content areas while maintaining alignment between curriculum content, learning objectives, and assessment questions. Current mapping processes are manual, time-intensive, and may miss important gaps or redundancies, leading to suboptimal exam preparation and curriculum-assessment misalignment.

### Proposed Solution

Integrated AI-powered curriculum mapping system that: (1) analyzes course content and learning objectives against USMLE Step 1 content outline, (2) maps Short-Answer Questions to specific course sessions and learning objectives, (3) identifies coverage gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities, (4) ensures comprehensive exam preparation, and (5) maintains curriculum-assessment coherence.

### Success Metrics

- Percentage of Step 1 content outline covered by curriculum
- Improved student Step 1 performance scores
- Percentage of learning objectives adequately assessed
- Reduced curriculum gaps and redundancies
- Enhanced curriculum planning efficiency
- Improved alignment between curriculum and assessment
- Reduced time for manual question and content mapping
