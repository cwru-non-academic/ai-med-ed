## Germ of the Idea

Comprehensive AI-powered admissions analytics platform that screens medical school applications, models applicant features for success prediction, and analyzes yield patterns using rich AAMC data to optimize admissions decisions and improve matriculation outcomes.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical school admissions processes rely heavily on manual application screening, lack systematic analysis of successful applicant characteristics, and struggle to predict and optimize yield rates. Current approaches miss opportunities to identify optimal candidates and understand enrollment patterns, leading to suboptimal class composition and inefficient resource allocation.

### Proposed Solution

Integrated AI platform that: (1) automatically screens applications using keyword analysis, essay evaluation, and rubric-based ranking, (2) identifies and models features of successful applicants based on historical interview decisions and performance outcomes, (3) analyzes AAMC end-of-cycle data to understand yield patterns and optimize enrollment strategies, (4) provides predictive modeling for applicant success, and (5) generates data-driven insights for admissions committee decision-making.

### Success Metrics

- Improved efficiency in application screening process
- Enhanced accuracy in identifying successful candidates
- Better prediction of student academic performance
- Increased yield rate optimization
- Reduced time to final admissions decisions
- Improved diversity and quality of incoming classes
- Enhanced understanding of applicant decision-making factors
