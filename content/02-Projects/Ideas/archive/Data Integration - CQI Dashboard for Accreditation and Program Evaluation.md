## Germ of the Idea

Build a centralized Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) dashboard to track and analyze key performance indicators related to medical education accreditation by integrating student performance data, faculty evaluations, and curriculum metrics with AI-driven trend analysis to highlight areas needing attention and generate real-time, LCME-aligned reports for data-driven continuous quality improvement.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical education programs struggle to maintain comprehensive oversight of accreditation requirements due to fragmented data systems, manual reporting processes, and difficulty identifying emerging issues before they become compliance problems. Current CQI processes are reactive rather than proactive, and LCME reporting is time-intensive and often based on outdated information.

This is a subset of the kind of integration and reporting requested in the [[Data Integration - Data Integration and Visualization]] project and should be considered closely with it.

### Proposed Solution

Centralized AI-powered dashboard that: (1) integrates all accreditation-relevant data sources, (2) continuously monitors LCME standards compliance, (3) uses AI to identify trends and early warning indicators, (4) generates real-time reports aligned with LCME requirements, (5) provides actionable recommendations for improvement, and (6) enables proactive quality management.

### Success Metrics

- Improved LCME compliance monitoring accuracy
- Reduction in time to identify and address quality issues
- Decreased preparation time for accreditation reporting
- Enhanced early warning system effectiveness
- Increased proactive vs. reactive improvement initiatives
