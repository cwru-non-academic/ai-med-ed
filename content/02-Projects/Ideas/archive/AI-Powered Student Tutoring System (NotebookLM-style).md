## Germ of the Idea

Implement NotebookLM-style AI tutoring system using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology to provide personalized learning assistance to medical students.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Students need personalized tutoring and learning support that is available 24/7, can adapt to individual learning needs, and can access comprehensive medical education content for contextualized assistance.

### Proposed Solution

AI tutoring system that uses RAG technology to provide personalized learning assistance by retrieving relevant information from course materials and generating contextual explanations, practice questions, and study guidance.

### Success Metrics

- Student usage and engagement metrics
- Improvement in academic performance
- Student satisfaction with tutoring quality
- Reduction in need for human tutoring resources

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Large Language Models for conversation and explanation
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system
- Document embedding and retrieval systems
- Personalization algorithms
- Learning progress tracking

### Data Requirements

- Course materials and textbooks
- Lecture notes and slides
- Practice questions and explanations
- Student learning history
- Performance data for personalization
