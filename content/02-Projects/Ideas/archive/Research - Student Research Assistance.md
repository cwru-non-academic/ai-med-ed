## Germ of the Idea

Develop comprehensive AI-powered research assistance platform for medical students, providing advanced literature search, analysis, and research support capabilities beyond existing subscription-based services.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical students conducting research projects lack access to sophisticated AI research tools, with existing solutions like Undermind requiring expensive subscriptions that may be prohibitive for student use.

### Proposed Solution

Institutional AI research assistant that provides literature search, analysis, hypothesis generation, methodology suggestions, and research gap identification specifically tailored for medical education research projects.

### Success Metrics

- Number of student research projects supported
- Quality of research outputs
- Time reduction in literature review processes
- Student satisfaction with research support
- Number of publications and presentations resulting

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Advanced literature search and retrieval systems
- Large Language Models for research assistance
- Citation analysis and recommendation engines
- Research methodology guidance systems
- Hypothesis generation algorithms

### Data Requirements

- Access to research databases and literature
- Institutional research project database
- Faculty research expertise mapping
- Student research project templates
- Best practice research methodologies
