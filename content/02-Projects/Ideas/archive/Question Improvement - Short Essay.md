## Germ of the Idea

AI system to analyze and improve existing Short Essay Questions (SEQ), focusing on quality enhancement and better integration with course content.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Existing SEQs may lack clarity, appropriate difficulty levels, or strong integration with learning objectives, leading to suboptimal assessment experiences.

### Proposed Solution

AI analysis of existing SEQs with recommendations for improvement in clarity, difficulty calibration, and curriculum integration, plus generation of enhanced versions.

### Success Metrics

- Improved question clarity scores
- Better alignment with learning objectives
- Enhanced student performance discrimination

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Question analysis algorithms
- Difficulty calibration models
- Content alignment assessment

### Data Requirements

- Existing SEQ database
- Student performance data
- Learning objectives mapping
