## Germ of the Idea

Generate Step 1 style multiple-choice questions for formative assessment, helping students prepare for licensing examinations while reinforcing course content.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Students need more practice with Step 1 style questions, but creating high-quality MCQs is time-intensive and requires specific expertise in exam formatting.

### Proposed Solution

AI system that generates Step 1 style multiple-choice questions based on course content and learning objectives, providing formative assessment opportunities.

### Success Metrics

- Student performance improvement on practice exams
- Alignment with actual Step 1 question styles
- Faculty time savings in question development

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Large Language Models trained on medical content
- Question template systems
- Medical knowledge validation

### Data Requirements

- Step 1 question format specifications
- Course content and learning objectives
- Medical knowledge databases
