## Germ of the Idea

AI system for grading Short-Answer Questions (SSEQ), generating new questions, and mapping questions to learning objectives and course sessions for better curriculum alignment.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Current short-answer questions lack proper mapping to learning objectives, grading is inconsistent, and generating aligned new questions is time-intensive.

### Proposed Solution

Multi-component AI system that: (1) grades existing SSEQs, (2) generates new questions aligned to specific learning objectives, (3) maps questions to course content for curriculum coherence.

### Success Metrics

- Improved alignment between questions and learning objectives
- Reduced time for question development
- Enhanced curriculum mapping coverage

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Large Language Models for question generation
- Curriculum mapping algorithms
- Automated grading with rubric alignment

### Data Requirements

- Existing question banks
- Learning objectives database
- Course session content
- Historical student responses
