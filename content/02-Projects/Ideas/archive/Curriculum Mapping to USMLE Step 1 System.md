## Germ of the Idea

Develop AI system to map institutional curriculum content to USMLE Step 1 content outline, ensuring comprehensive coverage and identifying gaps in exam preparation.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Medical schools struggle to ensure their curriculum adequately covers all USMLE Step 1 content areas, leading to potential gaps in student preparation and suboptimal exam performance.

### Proposed Solution

AI-powered curriculum mapping system that analyzes course content, learning objectives, and materials against the official USMLE Step 1 content outline to identify coverage gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities.

### Success Metrics

- Percentage of Step 1 content outline covered by curriculum
- Improved student Step 1 performance scores
- Reduced curriculum gaps and redundancies
- Enhanced curriculum planning efficiency

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Natural Language Processing for content analysis
- Curriculum mapping algorithms
- Gap analysis and reporting systems
- Content similarity assessment

### Data Requirements

- USMLE Step 1 content outline
- Course syllabi and learning objectives
- Lecture materials and content
- Assessment questions and topics
