## Germ of the Idea

Provide high-value feedback to students on Standardized Patient encounters using AI analysis of video and audio recordings from simulation center interactions.

## Overview

### Problem Statement

Current SP feedback is limited by human observer capacity and subjective interpretation, missing opportunities for detailed, consistent performance analysis.

### Proposed Solution

AI system analyzing video and audio from SP encounters to provide detailed feedback on communication skills, clinical technique, and professional behavior.

### Success Metrics

- Quality of feedback provided to students
- Consistency across different SP encounters
- Student improvement in clinical skills

## Literature Support

### Key Supporting Papers

- Reference to [FeedbackAssist]([https://feedbackassist.mcw.edu/](https://feedbackassist.mcw.edu/)) system at MCW as existing model

## Technical Requirements

### AI/ML Components Needed

- Computer vision for gesture/body language analysis
- Speech recognition and analysis
- Large Language Models for feedback generation
- Multimodal AI integration

### Data Requirements

- Video recordings from SP encounters
- Audio recordings of interactions
- Performance rubrics
- Historical feedback data

### Integration Points

*Note: These integration requirements are provisional and will be refined based on actual institutional systems and capabilities.*

- Simulation center recording and video management systems
- Standardized patient assessment and scheduling platform
- Video feedback system reference implementation (similar to FeedbackAssist)
